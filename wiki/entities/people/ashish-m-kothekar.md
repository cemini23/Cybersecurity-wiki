## Narrative

Ashish M Kothekar is the author of *Building a Next-Gen SOC with IBM QRadar* (Packt,
2023). Per the book's author bio, he is a long-tenured IBM engineer (16+ years)
working on IBM Security threat-management products — typically associated with the
**IBM SWAT (Software Architect Team) / SME path** for QRadar consulting +
implementation engagements. The book is an operator-focused architecture + deployment +
tuning reference rather than a detection-engineering recipe book; that framing is
reflected in @entities/tools/qradar.md (~36% extracted: strong on architecture, weak
on AQL idioms + rule patterns + apps).
