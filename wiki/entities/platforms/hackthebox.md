## Raw Concept

Corpus has Hackthebox e Vulnhub - Dicas e Truques.pdf and Red Team and Blue Team Labs and CTF.pdf anchoring this platform.

## Narrative

Online CTF + lab platform. Industry standard for hands-on pentest practice. [CONFIRMED]

**Surfaces:**
- **Machines** — individual hosts, retired weekly. Difficulty: Easy / Medium / Hard / Insane.
- **Pro Labs** — multi-host enterprise environments. Dante / Offshore / RastaLabs / Cybernetics. Closest sim to a real engagement; expensive but worth it for AD + lateral-movement practice.
- **HTB Academy** — structured paid courses + CPTS / CBBH / CWEE certifications, increasingly competitive with OffSec / eLearnSecurity offerings.
- **Sherlocks** — defensive scenarios (forensics, IR).

**OSCP prep:** the community-maintained TJ Null list of *OSCP-like* boxes is the standard warmup. The corpus's *OSCP Like Vulns Machines.pdf* extends + curates this list. See @entities/certifications/oscp.md.
