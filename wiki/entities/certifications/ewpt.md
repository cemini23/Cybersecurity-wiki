## Narrative

eLearnSecurity's web-app pentest cert. eWPT = mid-level (paired with the WAPT course); eWPTX = advanced (paired with WAPTX). [CONFIRMED]

**Format:** 7-day lab + 7-day report (eWPT); 14-day lab + 7-day report (eWPTX). Strong coverage of OWASP Top 10 + advanced web techniques (XXE, SSRF, deserialization, NoSQL injection, SAML attacks). Closest equivalent: OffSec's OSWA / OSWE.
