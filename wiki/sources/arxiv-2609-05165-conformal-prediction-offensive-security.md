## Narrative

Conformal prediction (CP) is widely used defensively but rarely applied to **offensive security** workflows. **K332** explores CP for offensive settings including privacy-preserving ML attacks and related domains — providing **distribution-free uncertainty sets** for attack outcomes. **Steal-from:** use CP to report calibrated success intervals on red-team evals rather than point ASR alone (pairs K277).
