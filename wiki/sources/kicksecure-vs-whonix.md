## Narrative

Same contributor family, different goals. Kicksecure: Debian hardening, **no** default Tor routing. Whonix: based on Kicksecure; **all** traffic through Tor via Gateway+Workstation. Kicksecure cannot provide Whonix’s anonymity guarantees. [CONFIRMED]
