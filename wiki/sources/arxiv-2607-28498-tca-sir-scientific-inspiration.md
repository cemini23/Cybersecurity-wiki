## Narrative

SIR bottleneck for LLM hypothesis generation: topical similarity misses **remote** inspirations that transfer a principle across domains. TCA (Target-Conditioned Abstraction) abstracts candidates relative to the target problem, then ranks transferable principles — closer to how humans use remote analogy.

### Steal (cyber / research ops)

1. Threat-intel / vuln-hypothesis search: rank by transferable principle to the target, not topic keywords alone
2. Pair with multi-hop KG patterns (@concepts/kamr-knowledge-aligned-multihop-retrieval.md)
3. Genealogy brick-walls: remote-analogy retrieval across towns/eras as method steal
