## Narrative

SLSA v1.0 organizes guarantees into a **build track** with levels L0–L3; the older spec used a single unnamed track SLSA 1–4. L0 = no guarantees. L1 = provenance exists (may be incomplete/unsigned; prevents mistakes, "trivial to bypass or forge"). L2 = **signed provenance generated by a hosted build platform** — "prevents tampering after the build through digital signatures." L3 = **hardened build platform** with controls that "prevent runs from influencing one another" and keep signing secrets "out of user-defined build steps" — "prevents tampering during the build — by insider threats, compromised credentials, or other tenants." [CONFIRMED slsa.dev]

Ingest note: cite the v1.0 build track, not the retired 1–4 ladder.
