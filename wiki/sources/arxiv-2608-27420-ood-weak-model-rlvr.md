## Narrative

**RLVR** improves LLM reasoning but often drops **policy entropy**, narrowing reasoning coverage and degrading **pass@k** for large k. Existing fixes use algorithmic regularization; the paper adds **cross-model non-parametric perturbation**: the target model generates answers based on **partial reasoning trajectories from a smaller, weaker model**. Those unfamiliar **prefixes** disrupt over-confidence and push the model down distinct reasoning paths. Across multiple math benchmarks the method beats vanilla RLVR, the gain grows with k, and it mitigates entropy collapse **without extra SFT, intricate reward designs, or complex prompting**.

**Why filed (OOD, no cyber adopt):** RL / math-reasoning training. **No trainer wire** — this is not adopted as a cybersecurity harness or a GRPO trainer.
