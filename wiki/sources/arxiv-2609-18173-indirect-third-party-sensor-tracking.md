## Narrative

Direct sensor access for vehicle tracking is often blocked by **ownership, privacy, cost, or operational constraints**, while third-party sensing assets may still leak indirect observations. **K345** models harnessing **indirect third-party sensor observations** when raw data is inaccessible. **Steal-from:** enumerate indirect observation surfaces in threat models; **authorized lab / written scope only** — no LIVE third-party tracking.
