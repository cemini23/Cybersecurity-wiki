## Narrative

Rapid review: 194 → 40 papers (2022–2025); dual screening Gwet AC1 0.76–0.83; thematic analysis → **eight competencies** for responsible LLM use in research. Domain expertise + oversight of AI outputs is dominant (Σn=123); AI literacy alone is a **risk factor when absent** without domain mastery.

### Eight competencies (salience order) [CONFIRMED]

1. Domain expertise and oversight of AI outputs (Σn=123)
2. Metacognition / decision making about when to use AI (55)
3. Ethics and academic integrity (53)
4. Prompt engineering for research (38)
5. Reproducibility / reporting of AI use (29)
6. Research planning and study design (lower frequency in abstract hierarchy)
7. AI literacy and technical knowledge (16 — risk when missing)
8. Analytical / exploratory support with AI (least frequent)

### Steal

1. Security research / CTI write-ups: treat **domain oversight** as non-delegable — LLM drafts need SME verification
2. Graduate / junior analyst training: teach metacognition + reproducibility before fancy prompt tricks
3. CCC harness: mirror competency checklist in research-agent eval rubrics
