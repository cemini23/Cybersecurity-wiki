## Narrative

Three linked-but-independent indeterminacies (**action / impact / user**) break assumptions baked into classic dual-use cyber + AI-ethics frameworks. Short-term industrialization of offense; long-term may democratize defense. Ops implication: autonomous offensive agents need explicit human accountability gates, scope attestation, and supply-chain disclosure for models/tools — not just "authorized use" boilerplate.
