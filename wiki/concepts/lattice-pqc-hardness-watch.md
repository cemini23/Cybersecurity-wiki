## Raw Concept

What changed in the worst-case hardness story for lattice problems that underwrite LWE-style PQC?

## Narrative

Hair–Sahai 2608.14529 give a **deterministic** (not randomized) poly-factor NP-hardness result for GapSVP in ℓp, p > 2. Advisory watch only: it does not yield a cryptanalytic break, a product wire, or a clone. Re-check if a follow-on paper claims an attack reduction.
