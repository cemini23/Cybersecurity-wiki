## Narrative

Social engineering = manipulating people to perform actions that compromise security. Categories: phishing (email), vishing (voice / phone), smishing (SMS), pretexting (false identity), tailgating (physical), baiting (USB drops / lures). For red team work, social engineering is typically the cheapest path to initial access. The corpus pairs this concept with @concepts/cyber-for-kids.md — the same techniques used offensively against employees appear in fake-profile WhatsApp scams against teens.
