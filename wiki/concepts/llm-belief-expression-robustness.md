## Raw Concept

Users persuade models via *how* they state beliefs (presupposition, evidentials, tone), not only what they claim. EoBench measures context-following vs prior knowledge across 19 linguistic types.

## Narrative

Relevant to prompt robustness and social-engineering-adjacent framing. Larger instruct models are not automatically more "truth-stubborn." No local adopt (EoB repo unlicensed).
