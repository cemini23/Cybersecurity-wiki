## Narrative

Sub-collection of the corpus dedicated to age-appropriate cyber-safety material for parents, teachers, school staff, and law-enforcement. Topics: cyberbullying recognition + response, fake-profile scams (WhatsApp / Instagram / Discord), sexual-predator + stalker awareness, school-attack survival (active-shooter pre-attack signal recognition + lockdown best practices). **Ethical framing matters:** these pages are written for *protective* context — parents talking with kids, teachers running classroom modules, LE officers building investigations. The pages should not be readable as a how-to for the inverse use case. Bilingual PT-BR + EN coverage across most topics. [CONFIRMED]
