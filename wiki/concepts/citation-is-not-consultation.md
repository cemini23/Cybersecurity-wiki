## Raw Concept

Question: **A citation is not a consultation** — operator steal from arXiv 2610.12361?

## Narrative

Naming the statute is the cheapest way to make a legal answer look grounded, and it is treated as evidence that the decision follows from that statute. It is not. Substitute the cited authority for an **unrelated one**, hold the facts fixed, and decode the verdict from **hidden states**: across seven open-weight models (8B-70B) and four judicial / contractual benchmarks, the verdict often does not follow the swap.

So a **stated ground** and an **operative ground** are different objects, and the model's own explanation is the wrong instrument for telling them apart. Audit with a **perturbation** — swap the authority and watch the decision — and prefer a hidden-state read over the model's account of why. Same shape as K415's rule-perturbation audit: both ask whether the thing the model says it used is the thing it actually used. Pairs K398 and K396.
