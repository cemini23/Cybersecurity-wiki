## Raw Concept

Long-context agents that copy distractors fail more. Reward shapes that bonus key-evidence overlap and penalize distractor overlap (GEAR) improve accuracy and reduce copy bloat.

## Narrative

Pair with pruning for tool-output-heavy agents. No public GEAR code at ingest.
